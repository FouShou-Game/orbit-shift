↑ / W moves outward one orbit; ↓ / S moves inward one orbit. No wrapping at the edges. 1 / 2 / 3 blink directly to the inner / middle / outer orbit. Check your landing position.
Chapter II projectiles: switch orbits just before contact for a Perfect Dodge. Its 0.15s window has a 1.25s cooldown. This opens an early counter. When bullet time is ready, it slows enemies for 0.6s while you move normally; bullet time has a 2.5s cooldown. Pass close to a projectile to graze it. Each projectile can reward one graze.
Closing Pages and Echo's walls, rings and cuts must be avoided through movement. They cannot trigger Perfect Dodge or graze rewards.
Echo: follow the rotating gap, line up with staggered rings, and switch only after the gold target lock. A blink does not protect you from dangerous landing positions.
Chapter II: Pursuit Cut locks one lane; Returning Spears cross all lanes and come back; Closing Pages squeeze inward. Reverse between the pages, then cross the mint exit on its marked orbit. Some cleared pages return once: "AGAIN." restores your position and the attack, with 0.3 seconds to prepare. The recalled page has a narrower exit. Health and completed progress stay with you. Chapter III progresses from single moves to chains to a rotating wall with locked cuts. Press E while the core is gold. Perfect Dodge against projectiles creates an early opening.
Chapter II restarts with 3 lives after defeat. Echo saves an anchor at each phase: a retry restores the lives you had at that anchor. Restart Echo begins phase 1 with 3 lives. Practice ends at its own result screen. P pauses.
