Controls & rules ▾
↑ / W moves outward one lane; ↓ / S moves inward one lane. No wrapping at the edges. 1 / 2 / 3 still select lanes directly.
Switch lanes just before a round bullet hits to trigger Perfect Dodge. Its 0.15s window has a 1.25s cooldown.
A successful dodge slows enemies for 0.6s; you keep moving normally. Skim bullets with your outer ring to graze.
Red striped fields are unblockable. Projectile rounds leave every lane available; field rounds have no extra bullets.
Two moves: Pursuit Cut chases along one locked lane; Returning Spears cross all lanes and come back along their marked paths. Press E while the core is gold. Perfect Dodge creates an immediate opening, even before a move finishes.
3 lives · 2.5s recovery after damage · P pauses · Click Play or the arena to begin.
